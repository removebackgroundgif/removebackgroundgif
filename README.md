## Why it is different

A GIF is not one image — it is a stack of frames sharing a palette and a timeline. Tools that only process the first frame produce a GIF that flickers back to its old background on frame two. RemoveBackgroundGIF keys **every frame**, re-encodes with the **original frame delays intact**, and previews the result on light and dark backgrounds before you export.

## FAQ

### Is RemoveBackgroundGIF really free?

Yes — no account, no watermark, no export limit. Color key runs locally in your browser, and the AI cutout is free as well.

### Are my files uploaded?

In color key mode, no — decoding, keying and encoding all happen in your browser tab. AI cutout sends each frame to the processing API, and nothing is stored.

### Does it keep every frame of the animation?

Yes — up to 1,000 frames and 50 MB per file, with the original frame timing preserved.

### Can I remove a white background from a GIF?

Yes — that is the default auto-detect case.

### Can I make a transparent GIF from a video?

Yes — drop an MP4 or WebM file on the [Transparent GIF Maker](https://removebackgroundgif.com/transparent-gif-maker) page and it is sampled into frames at the frame rate you choose.

### Can a GIF have partial transparency?

No — GIF transparency is 1-bit: each pixel is either fully transparent or fully opaque. Use APNG export for real 8-bit alpha with smooth edges.

### Why does my transparent GIF have white edges?

That is GIF's 1-bit transparency. Use edge shrink to cut away the anti-aliased rim, apply a matte color that matches the destination page, or export APNG instead.

### Why does my transparent GIF show a white box in some apps?

The app is flattening transparency onto white. Export with a matching matte color instead.

### What frame delay should I use?

100 ms per frame (10 fps) is the usual web default. Most chat apps clamp delays below 20 ms.

### What are the file limits?

50 MB and 1,000 frames per file.

### What formats can I export?

Animated GIF, APNG with full 8-bit alpha, a single PNG frame, or a ZIP of PNG frames.
